Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

The Builders' Ops Studio ("we," "us," or "our") operates thebuildersopsstudio.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Contact and inquiry forms -- name, email address, and any details you share when requesting a discovery call or downloading a resource.
  • Email communications -- messages you send us directly.

We also collect limited information automatically when you visit our site:

  • Pages visited, referring URL, browser type, and general location (city/region level) via Google Analytics.
  • Standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, page requested).

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule consultations.
  • To deliver resources you requested (e.g., the free operations audit).
  • To send follow-up communications about our services (you can opt out at any time).
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers we use to operate the business (e.g., Firebase/Google Cloud for data storage, email platforms). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Law or legal process when required by applicable law.

Cookies

We use Google Analytics cookies to understand aggregate site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings or opt out of Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Data Retention

We retain your information as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. You may request deletion at any time by emailing us.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at cynthia@thebuildersopsstudio.com.

Third-Party Links

Our site may link to third-party sites (e.g., Google Calendar). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The effective date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
